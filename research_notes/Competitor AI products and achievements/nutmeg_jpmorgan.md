# Nutmeg / J.P. Morgan Personal Investing (UK) and JPMorgan Group Wealth AI — Inventory for HSBC Wealth Competitor Benchmark

Research date: 23 September 2026. Scope: (a) Nutmeg / J.P. Morgan Personal Investing (JPMPI) / Chase UK items; (b) JPMorgan group-level wealth AI items likely to flow into UK retail wealth. Headline finding: **no source found shows Nutmeg/JPMPI publicly announcing any ML or GenAI customer-facing product.** Its "robo" proposition is rules-based/algorithmic automation plus human investment teams (and JPMAM for Smart Alpha). Group-level JPMorgan has substantial, well-documented AI (LLM Suite, Coach AI, IndexGPT, Evident #1), none of it publicly confirmed as deployed in the UK Nutmeg/JPMPI business.

---

## Q1. How does Nutmeg use algorithms/AI for portfolio construction, rebalancing, risk profiling, Smart Alpha, support/chatbots, onboarding?

### Takeaway
Nutmeg is a discretionary ETF-based digital wealth manager where portfolios are human-managed model portfolios mapped to risk levels; automation (onboarding questionnaire → risk level → model portfolio, automatic rebalancing) is algorithmic/rules-based, not publicly described as ML or GenAI. Smart Alpha is actively managed by J.P. Morgan Asset Management (JPMAM) research, not AI. No Nutmeg AI chatbot or GenAI support tool was found.

### Cited Findings — itemised inventory (a) Nutmeg / JPMPI / Chase UK

**Item A1 — Nutmeg core robo/discretionary platform (automated onboarding, risk levels, ETF model portfolios)**
- Date: founded 2011 by Nick Hungerford and William Todd — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nutmeg_(company)); one of the first direct-to-consumer robo-advice firms in the UK — [Citywire/Money Marketing via search; see Money Marketing](https://www.moneymarketing.co.uk/news/nutmeg-co-founder-nick-hungerford-dies-aged-43/)
- Operates as a discretionary investment manager using primarily ETFs; customers do not self-trade (pre-DIY) — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nutmeg_(company))
- Status: live (now under JPMPI brand).
- Classification: rules-based/algorithmic automation + human investment committee. Secondary/academic descriptions say Nutmeg "uses advanced algorithms to distribute and manage assets according to risk tolerance" and automatically rebalances — [ResearchGate paper (secondary, academic, low-authority)](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/384625676_Evaluating_the_Investment_Effectiveness_of_Robo-Advisers_A_Comparative_Analysis_of_User_Satisfaction_and_Nutmeg_Investment_Considering_Critical_Performance_Indicators). - Scale history: ~80,000 customers / £2bn AUM (Jan 2020) — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nutmeg_(company)); >140,000 customers / >£3.5bn AUM at JPM acquisition (June 2021, £700m) — [Money Marketing / Citywire search results](https://www.moneymarketing.co.uk/news/nutmeg-co-founder-nick-hungerford-dies-aged-43/); >200,000 clients / £4.5bn (Feb 2023) — [Chase UK](https://www.chase.co.uk/gb/en/media/chase-makes-investing-more-rewarding-with-nutmeg-link/); >265,000 investors / £8.5bn (Oct 2025) — [PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/partnerships/2025/wealth-management-platform-nutmeg-to-rebrand-as-j-p-morgan-personal-investing/); >270,000 clients / >£8.75bn (Nov 2025) — [search summary of fintech.global/ETF Express coverage](https://fintech.global/2025/11/14/j-p-morgan-launches-new-personal-investing-brand-in-u-k/) (minor discrepancy between sources reflects different dates).

**Item A2 — Smart Alpha portfolios "Powered by J.P. Morgan Asset Management"**

### Inferences
- For benchmarking, Nutmeg/JPMPI should be classed as "algorithmic automation + human/JPMAM active management + hybrid RM", not an AI-native wealth proposition. Its differentiator is JPMorgan parentage (JPMAM research, Chase UK distribution), not disclosed AI.
- The Wealth Planner + DIY + RM stack mirrors a classic "guidance funnel" and is the likely landing zone for any future group AI (e.g., Coach-style tools for RMs, GenAI support).

## Q2. Nutmeg's position on the FCA advice-vs-guidance perimeter; AI-driven guidance tools

### Takeaway
JPMPI offers a three-tier model: free financial guidance, paid restricted advice for all clients, and RMs for £250k+; the Wealth Planner is its digital guidance tool. No public statement found on FCA "targeted support" participation or AI-driven guidance.

### Cited Findings
- Relationship managers "complement the existing free financial guidance and the paid-for restricted advice service currently available to all clients" — [JPMPI](https://www.personalinvesting.jpmorgan.com/insights/introducing-jpmorgan-personal-investing)
- JPMPI Ltd FCA-authorised since 06/10/2011 (Nutmeg's original authorisation) — [FCA Register](https://register.fca.org.uk/s/firm?id=001b000000NMXASAA5)
- Wealth Planner described as a "digital guidance tool" with "tailored suggestions" — [JPMPI](https://www.personalinvesting.jpmorgan.com/insights/introducing-jpmorgan-personal-investing)

### Inferences
- "Tailored suggestions" in the Wealth Planner positions JPMPI close to the FCA's targeted support regime (in force from April 2026 per FCA policy—not verified in this research), but no source confirms JPMPI has opted in.

### Gaps
- No source found on JPMPI's targeted support application/launch or any AI-driven guidance; FCA regime timing not verified here.

## Q4. JPMorgan group wealth AI (section b)

### Takeaway
JPMorgan is the #1 bank in AI maturity (Evident, 4 years running) with LLM Suite (200k+ users), Coach AI for private-bank advisers (up to 95% faster information retrieval), IndexGPT (GPT-4 thematic indices) and ~$1.5bn stated AI value. These are US/global institutional-and-private-bank tools; none confirmed as deployed at JPMPI UK.

### Cited Findings — itemised inventory (b)

**Item B1 — LLM Suite (enterprise GenAI platform)**
- Launched summer 2024; 200,000 onboarded users within eight months; supports ideation and drafting; roadmap to integrate internal data and build agents for multi-step workflows; won American Banker 2025 Innovation of the Year Grand Prize (GenAI category), announced June 2025 — [JPMorganChase](https://www.jpmorganchase.com/about/technology/blog/llmsuite-ab-award)
- Model-agnostic, integrating OpenAI and Anthropic models; ~150,000 weekly users (secondary sources) — [AI News](https://www.artificialintelligence-news.com/news/jpmorgan-chase-ai-strategy-2025/) FLAG: secondary.
- GenAI toolkit on desktops of 200,000+ of ~320,000 employees, over half using it multiple times daily (May 2025) — [Reuters via Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/jpmorgan-says-ai-helped-boost-sales-add-clients-in-market-turmoil-4022588)
- Status: live (group-wide; applicability to UK staff incl. JPMPI likely but not confirmed).

**Item B2 — Coach AI (private client adviser co-pilot)**
- Reported 5 May 2025; advisers find content/research "up to 95% faster" (Mike Urciuoli, AWM CIO); Erdoes: tool "pre-populates all the data and the movement in real time, while also remembering clients' old investment preferences"; expected to help advisers expand client rosters 50% over 3–5 years; AWM gross sales +20% 2023–2024 with GenAI tools contributing; used during April 2025 tariff volatility; ~4,000 private-bank advisers — [Reuters via Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/jpmorgan-says-ai-helped-boost-sales-add-clients-in-market-turmoil-4022588); [Yahoo Finance/Reuters](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/jpmorgan-says-ai-helped-boost-170825647.html)
- Status: live (US private bank). Most plausible candidate to flow into JPMPI RM service.

**Item B3 — IndexGPT**
- Unveiled May 2024; thematic investment baskets built with OpenAI GPT-4 generating theme keywords, fed into an NLP model scanning news to find companies; targeted at institutional clients via swaps/notes — [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2024-05-03/jpmorgan-unveils-indexgpt-in-next-wall-street-bid-to-tap-ai-boom); [Finextra](https://www.finextra.com/newsarticle/44096/jpmorgan-unveils-indexgpt-investing-tool)
