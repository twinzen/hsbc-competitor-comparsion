# NatWest Group (NatWest, RBS, Ulster, Coutts, Premier/Wealth): AI products, solutions and achievements (as of 23 Sep 2026)

Scope note: this is an itemised inventory built from NatWest Group press releases and investor material, vendor pages and trade press. Items reported by only one secondary source are marked **[single secondary source]**. Figures from search-engine snippets whose page was not fetched in full are marked **[snippet]**.

## Q1. Cora / Cora+ virtual assistant and the NatWest–OpenAI collaboration

### Takeaway
Cora (launched 2017) got GenAI in 2024 as "Cora+", built with IBM watsonx. In March 2025 NatWest became the first UK bank to sign a strategic collaboration with OpenAI. In Q1 2026 an agentic assistant built on OpenAI models went live for 25,000 customers. Cora handled 7.1m conversations in H1 2026, and 3.8m of them were completed entirely digitally, up 23% year on year. In April 2026 NatWest also became the first UK bank with an app inside ChatGPT.

### Cited Findings
**Item 1: Cora digital assistant (Retail/Commercial, customer-facing, live since 2017)**
- Cora launched in 2017. It runs on the website, the mobile app and social media. It handled "nearly 11 million customer inquiries in the past year" (IBM case study, undated, c. 2024–25). — [IBM case study](https://ibm.com/case-studies/natwest)
- H1 2026: Cora handled 7.1 million conversations, of which 3.8 million were fulfilled entirely digitally, up 23% on H1 2025 (results published 31 Jul 2026). — [NatWest H1 2026 results](https://www.natwestgroup.com/news-and-insights/latest-stories/financial-reporting/2026/jul/h1-2026-natwest-group-results.html)

**Item 2: IBM–NatWest GenAI collaboration (announced 6 Nov 2023)**
- NatWest and IBM announced a GenAI initiative to upgrade Cora. — [IBM newsroom / PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/natwest-and-ibm-collaborate-on-generative-ai-initiative-to-enhance-customer-experience-301977636.html)

**Item 3: Cora+ (GenAI upgrade, launched June 2024, first as a 12-week pilot, since scaled)**
- Launched at London Tech Week in June 2024. NatWest was one of the first UK banks to deploy GenAI in a digital assistant. It was co-created on IBM watsonx (watsonx Assistant, IBM Cloud) and started as a 12-week pilot. — [NatWest PR Jun 2024](https://www.natwestgroup.com/news-and-insights/news-room/press-releases/data-and-technology/2024/jun/natwest-launches-cora-plus-the-latest-generative-ai-upgrade-to-t.html); [FinTech Futures](https://www.fintechfutures.com/partnerships/natwest-prepares-to-introduce-genai-powered-virtual-assistant-cora-in-collaboration-with-ibm)
- How it works: RAG over several secure sources (products, services, banking information). It answers directly in the chat instead of pointing customers elsewhere, and it summarises conversations and gives insights to human agents. — [IBM case study](https://ibm.com/case-studies/natwest)
- Result: "up to 150% improvement in satisfaction with some of the questions" (IBM). NatWest's own release puts it more broadly as "150% improvement in customer satisfaction" with less colleague intervention. **Note the difference in scope between the two claims.** — [IBM case study](https://ibm.com/case-studies/natwest); [NatWest–OpenAI PR, Mar 2025](https://www.natwestgroup.com/news-and-insights/news-room/press-releases/ai-and-data/2025/mar/natwest-open-ai-collaborate-to-accelerate-cutting-edge-ai-transf.html)
- GenAI-powered customer journeys in Cora grew from 4 to 21 during 2025. — [NatWest Group, Feb 2026](https://www.natwestgroup.com/news-and-insights/latest-stories/ai-and-data/2026/feb/being-a-trusted-partner-for-tomorrows-banking-through-technology.html)
- Date conflict: NatWest says June 2024. A FinTech Futures piece (seen only in a search snippet) says "launched in July last year". — [FinTech Futures](https://www.fintechfutures.com/ai-in-fintech/natwest-becomes-first-uk-bank-to-partner-with-openai-to-accelerate-ai-transformation) **[snippet]**

**Item 4: NatWest–OpenAI strategic collaboration (announced 20 Mar 2025)**
- First UK-headquartered bank to collaborate with OpenAI. The deal is bank-wide, covering the retail, commercial and wealth businesses. NatWest gets access to OpenAI products, an early view of upcoming products, and consultancy/executive input. Focus areas are digital assistants (Cora+, AskArchie+), identifying, reporting and resolving fraud and scams, financial planning/goal-setting, and complaints handling. At announcement NatWest had 275+ AI projects under exploration and about 25 use cases in production. Quotes came from Scott Marcar (CIO), Angela Byrne (Retail CEO) and Giancarlo Lionetti (OpenAI CCO). — [NatWest PR](https://www.natwestgroup.com/news-and-insights/news-room/press-releases/ai-and-data/2025/mar/natwest-open-ai-collaborate-to-accelerate-cutting-edge-ai-transf.html); [NatWest story](https://www.natwestgroup.com/news-and-insights/latest-stories/ai-and-data/2025/mar/accelerating-our-ai-transformation-in-banking-with-openai.html)

**Item 5: Agentic financial assistant in Cora (announced 17 Feb 2026, live for 25,000 customers by end of Q1 2026)**
- Built on OpenAI models. Customers ask natural-language questions about their recent spending in the app. NatWest described it as one of the first customer-facing agentic AI products from a UK bank. — [FStech, 17 Feb 2026](https://www.fstech.co.uk/fst/Thousands_Of_NatWest_Customers_To_Soon_Have_Access_To_Agentic_Financial_Assistant.php); [NatWest Group, Feb 2026](https://www.natwestgroup.com/news-and-insights/latest-stories/ai-and-data/2026/feb/being-a-trusted-partner-for-tomorrows-banking-through-technology.html); [Tech Monitor](https://www.techmonitor.ai/ai-and-automation/natwest-openai-boost-ai-customer-support/)
- The H1 2026 results refer to "the first agentic AI assistant". — [NatWest H1 2026 results](https://www.natwestgroup.com/news-and-insights/latest-stories/financial-reporting/2026/jul/h1-2026-natwest-group-results.html) (wording via [Insider Media](https://www.insidermedia.com/news/south-westnational/natwest-profits-up-by-a-fifth-after-ai-led-tech-drive-and-wealth-focus))

**Item 6: Agentic fraud support in Cora (announced Feb 2026, being rolled out in 2026)**
- Next-generation fraud support: customers report and resolve fraud cases in real time through natural-language conversation. CIO.inc says the aim is to remove the need for a human agent. — [FStech](https://www.fstech.co.uk/fst/Thousands_Of_NatWest_Customers_To_Soon_Have_Access_To_Agentic_Financial_Assistant.php); [CIO.inc](https://www.cio.inc/bank-that-ripped-up-playbook-for-ai-a-31786)

**Item 7: Voice-to-voice AI experiment (announced Feb 2026 for later in 2026, experimental)**
- Voice responses with "human-like empathy, tone and inflection". — [FStech](https://www.fstech.co.uk/fst/Thousands_Of_NatWest_Customers_To_Soon_Have_Access_To_Agentic_Financial_Assistant.php)

**Item 8: NatWest app in ChatGPT for home-buying and remortgage guidance (launched late Apr 2026, live)**
- First UK bank with an app in ChatGPT. It uses NatWest APIs so users can calculate how much they could borrow, test affordability and deposit scenarios, and see tailored rates. It is open to customers and non-customers, who are then signposted to NatWest channels (appointments, digital applications). It sits in the ChatGPT app store alongside Rightmove and MoneySuperMarket. — [NatWest PR Apr 2026](https://www.natwestgroup.com/news-and-insights/news-room/press-releases/ai-and-data/2026/apr/natwest-becomes-first-uk-bank-to-launch-trusted-home-buying-advi.html); [Finextra](https://www.finextra.com/newsarticle/47678/natwest-embeds-home-buying-options-on-chatgpt); [Mortgage Solutions, 30 Apr 2026](https://www.mortgagesolutions.co.uk/mortgage-news/2026/04/30/natwest-launches-home-buying-advice-in-chatgpt/)

### Inferences
- NatWest's customer AI moved in steps: rules-based Cora (2017), then GenAI with IBM (2024), then agentic features on OpenAI models (2026). This looks like a vendor shift from IBM toward OpenAI. No source says IBM was dropped.
- The ChatGPT app is an early "distribution inside third-party AI" move. HSBC has no stated equivalent in these sources.

### Gaps
- No public figures yet on the agentic assistant's results (satisfaction, containment, accuracy), and no confirmation it scaled beyond 25,000 customers.
- No usage figures for the ChatGPT mortgage app.
- It is unclear whether NatWest uses ChatGPT Enterprise for colleagues. Sources mention an internal LLM platform and Copilot Chat, not ChatGPT Enterprise.

## Q2. AI in Coutts and wealth (Private Banking & Wealth Management)

### Takeaway
Public detail on wealth AI is thin. The main primary source is the PB&WM Investor Spotlight (26 Jun 2025). It says AI call and meeting summarisation saves up to 15 minutes per client meeting, bankers have GenAI through the internal chatbot, and the CRM is moving to Microsoft Dynamics so AI can generate client insight. By Feb 2026 NatWest said AI summarisation had freed about 30% more relationship-manager time for clients. No sources describe a named Coutts suitability, onboarding or Consumer Duty AI tool.

### Cited Findings
**Item 9: AI call/meeting summarisation for Coutts/PB&WM bankers (live by Jun 2025)**
- "We are using AI to summarise calls in order to free up our people's time. This saves up to 15 minutes for every client meeting that takes place. We also give our people access to Generative AI through our internal chatbot" (Jamel Oulidi, PB&WM COO). — [PB&WM Investor Spotlight transcript, 26 Jun 2025](https://www.investors.rbs.com/~/media/Files/R/RBS-IR-V2/documents/natwest-group-private-banking-wealth-management-spotlight-transcript-260625.pdf)
- Feb 2026: AI summarisation for Relationship Managers "freed 30% more time" in Private Banking/Wealth. Trade press paraphrased this as "advisers now spend 30% more of their time in direct client conversation", using AI for meeting summaries, document compilation and correspondence analysis. — [NatWest Group, Feb 2026](https://www.natwestgroup.com/news-and-insights/latest-stories/ai-and-data/2026/feb/being-a-trusted-partner-for-tomorrows-banking-through-technology.html); [AI News](https://www.artificialintelligence-news.com/news/banking-ai-in-multiple-business-functions-at-natwest/); [CIO.inc](https://www.cio.inc/bank-that-ripped-up-playbook-for-ai-a-31786)
- In PB&WM, AI is also used to improve document management and to build bespoke client content. — [AI News](https://www.artificialintelligence-news.com/news/banking-ai-in-multiple-business-functions-at-natwest/) **[snippet; single secondary source]**

**Item 10: PB&WM workflow automation and AI-ready CRM (announced Jun 2025)**
- Microsoft Dynamics workflow, the Group standard. The first implementation was due to go live in summer 2025 and would cut the time to deliver simple advice from 7 days to 2. Next steps are complex advice, onboarding and faster lending decisions. — [PB&WM Spotlight transcript](https://www.investors.rbs.com/~/media/Files/R/RBS-IR-V2/documents/natwest-group-private-banking-wealth-management-spotlight-transcript-260625.pdf)
- The CRM migrates to Microsoft Dynamics in 2026 and links to the Group CRM. This "means we'll be able to use AI to unlock greater insight into client needs and preferences". Strategy is to deploy "technology, data and generative AI to increase operational efficiency" and to cut the PB&WM cost:income ratio from 74% (2024) to the mid-60s by 2027. — [PB&WM Spotlight transcript](https://www.investors.rbs.com/~/media/Files/R/RBS-IR-V2/documents/natwest-group-private-banking-wealth-management-spotlight-transcript-260625.pdf)
- COO Oulidi: "You overlay AI and all of the data work, and that gives you leads... top of the funnel pretty much automatically". — [same transcript](https://www.investors.rbs.com/~/media/Files/R/RBS-IR-V2/documents/natwest-group-private-banking-wealth-management-spotlight-transcript-260625.pdf)

**Item 11: Digital wealth manager / online investment advice for Retail/Premier (launched Mar 2025, live)**
- Online advice for a one-off £200 fee, charged only if the customer invests. It is a virtual screen-share session with a "digital wealth manager", described as "automated advice". The service is being scaled, with personalised prompts through the app. **This is a digital/automated advice service. Sources do not explicitly call it AI.** — [PB&WM Spotlight transcript](https://www.investors.rbs.com/~/media/Files/R/RBS-IR-V2/documents/natwest-group-private-banking-wealth-management-spotlight-transcript-260625.pdf)
- Context: Coutts was recognised in 2024 as the UK's best domestic private bank (NPS 49) and best UK private bank for digital customer experience. — [same transcript](https://www.investors.rbs.com/~/media/Files/R/RBS-IR-V2/documents/natwest-group-private-banking-wealth-management-spotlight-transcript-260625.pdf)

**Item 12: Evelyn Partners acquisition (completed by H1 2026; wealth context, not AI)**
- AUMA reached £130.6bn. H1 2026 set a record for AUM net inflows at £2.0bn, and 45,000 customers invested for the first time. Bestinvest platform trials with retail customers are under way. — [NatWest H1 2026 results](https://www.natwestgroup.com/news-and-insights/latest-stories/financial-reporting/2026/jul/h1-2026-natwest-group-results.html); [Coutts](https://www.coutts.com/insights/investing/coutts-welcomes-evelyn-partners.html)

### Inferences
- NatWest's wealth AI centres on relationship-manager productivity (summarisation, internal GenAI, CRM insight), not client-facing AI. That makes it directly comparable with HSBC's wealth RM copilots.
- The 2026 Fintech Programme includes Aveni, a UK firm focused on AI for financial advice (Item 22). This may point to interest in advice-note and suitability AI, but no deployment is confirmed.

### Gaps
- No public source found for Coutts-specific AI in suitability automation, client onboarding/KYC, Consumer Duty controls, or a named meeting-intelligence vendor.
- No Coutts.com press item on AI was found.
- Evelyn Partners AI integration is unknown.

## Q3. Colleague GenAI tools, coding, fraud, complaints, call summarisation, personalisation

### Takeaway
All 60,000 colleagues have AI tools: an internal GenAI platform, Microsoft Copilot Chat and AskArchie+. More than 12,000 engineers use AI coding tools, which write about 35% of the bank's code. Agentic engineering trials delivered a claimed 10x productivity gain. Retail call and complaint summarisation has saved 70,000+ hours.

## Q4. Achievements, governance, partnerships, hires and cost savings

### Takeaway
Governance and partnership activity is strong: OpenAI; AWS and Accenture (5 years); a Chief AI Research Officer; a mandatory AI ethics accreditation for 60,000 staff; FCA AI Live Testing; and co-authorship of the agentic commerce principles. Stated benefits include £1.2bn of 2025 tech/AI spend, about £100m of investment capacity freed through engineering productivity, and cost:income improvement in H1 2026. In the Evident AI Index NatWest ranks mid-table, below HSBC.

### Cited Findings
**Item 21: AWS + Accenture 5-year data and AI collaboration (announced 23 Jul 2025)**
- Consolidates data into a single bank-wide platform enabled by AI. It covers personalisation for about 20 million customers plus back-office work (security, capital management, regulatory reporting). — [NatWest PR](https://www.natwestgroup.com/news-and-insights/news-room/press-releases/ai-and-data/2025/jul/natwest-group-to-accelerate-bank-wide-data-and-ai-transformation.html); [AWS press](https://press.aboutamazon.com/aws/2025/7/natwest-group-to-accelerate-bank-wide-data-and-ai-transformation-through-5-year-collaboration-with-accenture-and-aws); [The Register](https://www.theregister.com/2025/07/23/natwest_aws_accenture_deal/)

**Item 22: 2026 Fintech Programme, AI-themed (announced May 2026)**
- Theme: "How AI is Shaping the Future of Customer Experience", a 12-week programme. The cohort is Aveni, Condukt, DeepFlow, Empath_AI, Galveston Group, Gradient Labs, Murphy AI and Round Treasury. — [NatWest PR May 2026](https://www.natwestgroup.com/news-and-insights/news-room/press-releases/ai-and-data/2026/may/natwest-announces-firms-selected-for-its-2026-fintech-programme.html); [Finextra](https://www.finextra.com/newsarticle/47737/natwest-selects-eight-ai-driven-fintechs-for-2026-innovation-programme)
