# Citigroup (Citi) – AI Products, Solutions and Achievements (focus: Citi Wealth / Citi Private Bank)

Research current to 23 September 2026. Every fact is tied to a source URL. "Single secondary source" flags mark items not confirmed by Citi primary material.

## 1. Wealth / Private Bank AI tools (AskWealth, Advisor Insights, Portfolio Intelligence, CitiScribe, Client 360, AskWealth CIO, Citi Sky, CIO agentic AI, Citi Wealth Builder)

### Takeaway
Citi Wealth (Citi Private Bank, Citigold, Citi Private Client, Wealth at Work) has built an AI stack in stages. Advisor-facing generative AI came first: AskWealth (live globally since Aug 2025, first launched in Asia) and Advisor Insights (Aug 2025 pilot). Four more tools followed in Apr 2026: Portfolio Intelligence, AskWealth CIO, Client 360 and CitiScribe. The flagship client-facing launch is **Citi Sky**, an AI "team member" with a voice and video avatar, built on Google Gemini and DeepMind. It was unveiled on 22 Apr 2026 and started a phased US Citigold rollout in summer 2026. Most of these tools launched in North America first. The only confirmed global or international Private Bank rollout dates are for AskWealth (global), Advisor Insights (international colleagues, Q4 2025–Q1 2026) and Portfolio Intelligence (Private Bank globally, Q2 2026). I found nothing UK-specific.

### Cited Findings – itemised inventory

**1.1 AskWealth (generative AI assistant for advisors and service teams)** — announced 25 Aug 2025; area: Wealth (all segments); status: LIVE globally
- A generative-AI conversational assistant that gives quick answers from across the Wealth ecosystem. It resolves client inquiries and supplies market insights and research to advisors. It went live globally after launching first in Asia — [Citi press release, 25 Aug 2025](https://www.citigroup.com/global/news/press-release/2025/citi-wealth-launches-advisor-insights-askwealth)
- Service teams use it to pull answers from data repositories that range from market insights to client-specific portfolio data — [Banking Dive](https://www.bankingdive.com/news/citi-wealth-generative-ai-ml-banking-platforms/758847/)
- Joe Bonanno (Head of Data, Analytics & Innovation, Citi Wealth): "These platforms will save hours of time for our advisors, bankers and service teams…" No usage metrics or vendor were disclosed — [Citi press release](https://www.citigroup.com/global/news/press-release/2025/citi-wealth-launches-advisor-insights-askwealth)

**1.2 Advisor Insights (AI/ML advisor dashboard)** — announced 25 Aug 2025; area: Wealth; status: PILOT, then expanding
- A tailored dashboard that sends advisors timely messages about market moves, portfolios and events, with content alerts and Chief Investment Office (CIO) insights. The pilot ran with Citigold and Citi Private Client North America advisors. Citi said it would expand to North America Citi Private Bankers and **international colleagues in Q4 2025–Q1 2026** — [Citi press release](https://www.citigroup.com/global/news/press-release/2025/citi-wealth-launches-advisor-insights-askwealth)
- Jane Fraser named "Citi Wealth Advisor Insights" on the Q2 2026 earnings call (Jul 2026) as an example of AI helping Citi bring products to market faster — [Motley Fool Q2 2026 transcript](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/07/21/citi-c-q2-2026-earnings-call-transcript/)

**1.3 Portfolio Intelligence (client-facing portfolio insights)** — announced 9 Apr 2026; area: Wealth / Private Bank; status: LIVE (Citi Private Bank North America), with global rollout planned
- Gives clients a single view that combines positions, performance and Citi Wealth CIO market insights. Through an app, advisors can send personalised messages. It was already live for Citi Private Bank clients in North America, with expansion planned to **Citi Private Bank clients globally in Q2 2026** and to all Wealth clients by year-end 2026. Technology partners named in the release: Google Cloud and Palantir — [Citi press release, 9 Apr 2026](https://www.citigroup.com/global/news/press-release/2026/citi-wealth-deploys-ai-powered-technology-to-enhance-client-experience)

**1.4 AskWealth CIO** — announced 9 Apr 2026; area: Wealth; status: PILOT
- An extension of AskWealth. Advisors use a chat interface to get CIO views sourced from the CIO's latest reports. It was piloting in Citigold, Wealth at Work and Citi Private Bank North America, with other regions to follow "in the following weeks" — [Citi press release, 9 Apr 2026](https://www.citigroup.com/global/news/press-release/2026/citi-wealth-deploys-ai-powered-technology-to-enhance-client-experience)

**1.5 Client 360** — announced 9 Apr 2026; area: Wealth; status: PILOT, then rolling out
- A unified internal platform that shows holdings, positions, call logs, opportunities and interests on one screen. It was piloting with selected Citi Private Bank and Wealth at Work advisors in North America. Full rollout to those segments was planned for April 2026, and all Citi Wealth colleagues were due to get access in Q2 2026 — [Citi press release](https://www.citigroup.com/global/news/press-release/2026/citi-wealth-deploys-ai-powered-technology-to-enhance-client-experience); [Banking Dive](https://www.bankingdive.com/news/citi-wealth-generative-ai-ml-banking-platforms/758847/)

**1.6 CitiScribe (AI meeting notes)** — rolled out Q1 2026, announced 9 Apr 2026; area: Wealth; status: LIVE (all Wealth advisors in North America)
- AI note-taking that summarises client discussions and key points, removing manual documentation for advisors and their teams. It was rolled out to all Wealth advisors in North America in Q1 2026. No rollout outside North America was disclosed — [Citi press release](https://www.citigroup.com/global/news/press-release/2026/citi-wealth-deploys-ai-powered-technology-to-enhance-client-experience)
- Coverage: [PYMNTS "Citi Arms Wealth Advisors With 4 AI Tools"](https://www.pymnts.com/artificial-intelligence-2/2026/citi-arms-wealth-advisors-with-4-ai-tools-to-cut-busywork/); [FinTech Magazine](https://fintechmagazine.com/news/citi-wealth-rolls-out-ai-tools-for-advisers-and-client-teams)

**1.7 Citi Sky (AI "member of the Citi Wealth team", client-facing, voice and video avatar)** — unveiled 22 Apr 2026 at Google Cloud Next 2026; area: Wealth (Citigold); status: phased rollout to US Citigold clients from summer 2026
- An always-on AI assistant that answers clients' financial questions in real time, gives market insights from the Wealth CIO and flags events such as CD maturities. It works alongside human advisors. It was built on Google Cloud's **Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform** using **Google DeepMind real-time avatar technology** and the **Gemini Live API**, with Google Cloud Forward Deployed AI Engineers and the DeepMind Applied team. It launches in English and Spanish, with more languages planned. Andy Sieg: "We believe Citi Sky will change the model of wealth management… With Citi Sky, you simply ask – act." No metrics were given — [Citi press release, 22 Apr 2026](https://www.citigroup.com/global/news/press-release/2026/citi-wealth-unveils-citi-sky-ai-powered-member-google-cloud-deepmind-technologies); [Google Cloud press corner](https://www.googlecloudpresscorner.com/2026-04-22-Citi-Wealth-Unveils-Citi-Sky-An-AI-Powered-Member-of-the-Citi-Wealth-Team,-Built-Using-Google-Cloud-and-Google-DeepMind-Technologies)
- At Investor Day (7 May 2026), Sieg called Citi Sky "a new intelligence layer, conversational, actionable and secure" that will "make our advisors and relationship managers vastly more productive". He said it would roll out "this summer to Citigold clients in the U.S., with a broader introduction to follow" — [Citi Investor Day 2026 Wealth transcript](https://www.citigroup.com/rcs/citigpa/storage/public/investor-day/citi-ID26-wealth-presentation-transcript.pdf)

**1.8 Agentic AI in the Wealth CIO office and the four-layer Wealth AI platform** — Investor Day, 7 May 2026; area: Wealth / Private Bank; status: in use (no metrics disclosed)
- Sieg said CIO Kate Moore's team is "layering agentic AI directly on to Citi's proprietary research and models", creating "a step change in how quickly we move from investment thesis to portfolio construction". **1.9 Wealth-banker meeting-preparation agents (on Arc)** — 30 Apr 2026; status: announced as an example use case
- Citi gave "wealth banking agents that prepare client meetings (gathering portfolio data, analysing market trends, modelling scenarios)" as an example of agents built on its Arc platform — [Citi Perspectives, "Introducing AI Agents", 30 Apr 2026](https://www.citigroup.com/global/news/perspectives/2026/introducing-ai-agents-next-phase-citi-artificial-intelligence-journey)

## 2. Enterprise generative AI and colleague tools (Citi Assist, Citi Stylus, Stylus Workspaces, agentic AI, Arc, developer AI, training, AI Champions)

### Takeaway
Citi runs its own internal generative AI suite. Citi Assist and Citi Stylus launched in Dec 2024. Stylus Workspaces uses Gemini and Claude models and gained agentic features in Sep 2025 and a team feature called Spaces in Feb 2026. In Apr 2026 Citi launched Arc, an enterprise platform for building AI agents. About 180,000 staff in 80+ countries have access, and more than 80% (Q1 2026) to nearly 90% (Q2 2026) use the tools. For developers, Citi uses GitHub Copilot, Cognition's Devin and its in-house Citi Squad code-review tool. Citi says this frees about 100,000 developer hours a week.

### Cited Findings – itemised inventory

**2.1 Citi Assist** — Dec 2024; colleague tools; LIVE
- A virtual assistant that answers policy and procedural questions. It launched alongside Citi Stylus to about 140,000 employees in 8 countries (US, Canada, Hungary, India, Ireland, Poland, Singapore, UK) — [Fortune, 9 Apr 2025](https://fortune.com/2025/04/09/how-citis-cto-is-rolling-out-new-gen-ai-productivity-tools-to-more-employees-across-the-globe); [HR Grapevine, Dec 2024](https://www.hrgrapevine.com/us/content/article/2024-12-06-a-super-smart-coworker-at-your-fingertips-citigroup-rolls-out-ai-productivity-tools-for-staff)
- Guidance covers compliance, risk, HR and finance policies — [HR Dive](https://www.hrdive.com/news/citi-ai-prompt-training-requirement/802862/)

**2.2 Citi Stylus** — Dec 2024; colleague tools; LIVE
- Summarises documents, translates, and compares several documents at once. By Apr 2025 it had expanded to 11 countries, and CTO David Griffiths expected a "large majority" of the roughly 230k workforce to get access — [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2025/04/09/how-citis-cto-is-rolling-out-new-gen-ai-productivity-tools-to-more-employees-across-the-globe)

**2.3 Citi Stylus Workspaces** — first introduced Dec 2024; agentic upgrade announced 23–24 Sep 2025; colleague tools; LIVE, with the agentic features first piloted
- A proprietary platform that uses **Google Gemini and Anthropic Claude** models — [Banking Dive](https://www.bankingdive.com/news/citi-deploys-agentic-AI-tools-stylus-workspaces/760868/); [CIO Dive](https://www.ciodive.com/news/citi-agentic-AI-tools-stylus-workspaces/760804/)
- The agentic upgrade connects to internal data, the employee directory, project-management tools and web search, and runs multi-step workflows. Citi's example: one prompt identifies the top five US branded-card businesses, summarises their strategies and translates the result into Spanish. The rollout began in Sep 2025 with thousands of employees, then expanded in stages — [Citi press release](https://www.citigroup.com/global/news/press-release/2025/citi-unveils-citi-stylus-workspaces-agentic-ai-turbocharging-productivity)
- The pilot group was 5,000 employees for up to 6 weeks, with input limits to control cost. Griffiths: "Does it mean that we need less people? I don't know. It certainly means that we would get a lot more done." — [The Register, 24 Sep 2025](https://www.theregister.com/2025/09/24/citi_pilots_agentic_ai/)

**2.4 Stylus Workspaces "Spaces" (team AI collaboration)** — 4 Feb 2026; PILOT
- Lets teams of up to 15 work jointly on documents, analyses and reports in a secure AI workspace, with Jira integration. The pilot had 250 employees, with plans to extend to all 182,000 Stylus Workspaces users. Models: Gemini and Claude — [Banking Dive](https://www.bankingdive.com/news/citi-pilots-ai-assisted-workspace-employees/811339/)

**2.5 Arc (enterprise AI agent platform)** — Apr 2026 (announced 30 Apr–1 May 2026); firm-wide; LIVE for developers
- A central platform for building and scaling AI agents within Citi's risk framework. Developers use it first; agents will later be embedded in everyday employee workflows. Citi: "Every agent will be monitored, auditable and governed." Its stated aim is to be "the most AI-empowered financial institution in the world" — [Citi Perspectives, 30 Apr 2026](https://www.citigroup.com/global/news/perspectives/2026/introducing-ai-agents-next-phase-citi-artificial-intelligence-journey)
- CTO David Griffiths: "For the first time, we can deploy embedded AI agents at enterprise scale across every business line, every geography, every function." No vendor or model was named — [CIO Dive](https://www.ciodive.com/news/citi-launches-arc-scale-ai-agents/819113/). An Axios exclusive exists but could not be retrieved: https://www.axios.com/2026/04/30/exclusive-citi-moves-into-agentic-ai

**2.6 Developer AI: GitHub Copilot, Citi Squad (AI code review), Cognition Devin**
- In early 2025 Citi gave about 30,000 developers generative AI coding tools — [CIO Dive](https://www.ciodive.com/news/citi-bank-modernization-generative-ai-coding-assistant/737624/)
- Jul 2025: agentic AI (Cognition's Devin) and GitHub Copilot were being rolled out to about 40,000 developers. Figures reported: 740,000 automated code reviews by Citi Squad, an in-house generative AI tool launched in 2024; about 100,000 hours saved a week; 5–15% productivity gain for Copilot users; 2–20x speed-up on some agentic tasks. Griffiths: "We're certainly not allowing any kind of deployment of code by agents." — [American Banker, 14 Jul 2025](https://www.americanbanker.com/news/citi-is-rolling-out-agentic-ai-to-its-40-000-developers)

- Oct 2025 (Q3 2025 earnings): Jane Fraser said AI frees up **100,000 hours of weekly capacity for developers**, and that about 180,000 employees in 83 countries have access to internal AI tools — [Global Banking & Finance (Reuters syndication)](https://www.globalbankingandfinance.com/citigroup-ai-two/)

**2.7 Mandatory AI prompt training ("Asking Smart Questions – Prompting like a Pro")** — late Sep / 1 Oct 2025
- Mandatory for about 175,000 employees in about 80 locations, to be completed within 60 days. It takes under 10 minutes for experts and about 30 minutes for beginners. By then employees had logged more than 6.5 million prompts. The memo came from Tim Ryan (Head of Technology & Business Enablement) and Anand Selva (COO) — [HR Dive](https://www.hrdive.com/news/citi-ai-prompt-training-requirement/802862/); [American Banker](https://www.americanbanker.com/news/citi-mandates-ai-prompt-training-for-most-employees)

- A network of about 4,000 volunteers across 84 countries who coach peers and feed back on tools. Adoption was reported at over 70% of about 182,000 staff with access. *Flag: secondary sources only* — [The HR Digest](https://www.thehrdigest.com/a-team-of-ai-accelerators-at-citi-is-redefining-ai-adoption-in-the-workplace/); [Kavout](https://www.kavout.com/market-lens/is-citigroup-s-ai-survival-plan-a-defensive-play-or-a-growth-strategy)

**2.9 AI-assisted performance reviews** — Oct 2025
- Bloomberg headline: "Citi Looks to AI to Help Bosses Write Performance Reviews" (28 Oct 2025). I saw only the headline because of the paywall — [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-10-28/citi-looks-to-ai-to-help-bosses-write-performance-reviews)

**2.11 Google Cloud strategic partnership** — 28 Oct 2024; multi-year
- Covers migrating workloads and applications to Google Cloud and using **Vertex AI** (including Gemini) for generative AI in developer toolkits, document processing and digitisation for customer-servicing teams — [Citi press release](https://www.citigroup.com/global/news/press-release/2024/citi-and-google-cloud-announce-strategic-agreement); [CIO Dive](https://www.ciodive.com/news/citi-google-cloud-partnership-app-migration-ai-modernization/731260/)

### Inferences
- Citi uses several model vendors: Google (Gemini/Vertex), Anthropic (Claude) in Stylus Workspaces, Meta Llama in Sales Assist (see 3.2), and GitHub/Microsoft Copilot and Cognition Devin for developers. No direct OpenAI deployment was found.
- Adoption moved from access (140k in Dec 2024) to regular use (80% in Q1 2026, about 90% in Q2 2026). This suits a headline benchmark comparison with HSBC.

## 3. Client-facing AI and business-line AI (Services/Treasury, US Personal Banking, Cards, Markets)

### Takeaway
Outside Wealth, Citi's main AI deployments are in Services, built around five pillars (Agent Assist, Sales Assist, Client Assist on CitiDirect, Ops Assist / Intelligent Document Processing and others) with 50+ use cases. Other areas are US Personal Banking servicing (Agent Assist), card underwriting, and document processing in Markets.
