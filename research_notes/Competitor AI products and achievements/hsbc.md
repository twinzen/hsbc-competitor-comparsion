# HSBC – AI Products, Solutions and Achievements (baseline for wealth competitor benchmark)

Current as of 23 September 2026. Every fact carries a source. "Single source" = only one secondary outlet found. Page contents were read through a summarising fetch tool, so exact quotes should be spot-checked before external publication.

## 1. Wealth AI (Wealth & Personal Banking / Global Private Banking / Premier / Asset Management)

### Takeaway
"Wealth Intelligence" is **verified** as a real HSBC product. It is an internal GenAI platform built on OpenAI's LLM, deployed 16 Sep 2025 for Private Bank and wealth staff in Hong Kong and Singapore and extended to Malaysia in Sep 2026. It supports RMs and does not give advice to clients. The June 2026 Google Cloud deal adds "hyper-personalised wealth management support" and an RM "decision assistant" (prep time cut "from hours to minutes") as the next wave. HSBC's AI indices (AiGT, AiPEX, AiMAX) sit in Global Banking & Markets, not Asset Management. HSBC Asset Management's AI activity found so far is venture investing (Model ML).

### Itemised inventory

**W1. Wealth Intelligence (HSBC Private Bank / Global Private Banking & wealth)**
- Date: announced 16 Sep 2025 — [HSBC Private Bank](https://www.privatebanking.hsbc.com/media-releases-and-news/hsbc-deploys-wealth-intelligence/) (also on the [HK PB site](https://www.asia.privatebanking.hsbc.com.hk/media-releases-and-news/hsbc-deploys-wealth-intelligence/)).
- Markets: Hong Kong and Singapore first, with plans to scale to more markets globally — [HSBC Private Bank](https://www.privatebanking.hsbc.com/media-releases-and-news/hsbc-deploys-wealth-intelligence/)
- Users: client-facing wealth staff, investment counsellors and product specialists — [HSBC Private Bank](https://www.privatebanking.hsbc.com/media-releases-and-news/hsbc-deploys-wealth-intelligence/)
- Technology: built in-house by HSBC developers on OpenAI's LLM — [HSBC Private Bank](https://www.privatebanking.hsbc.com/media-releases-and-news/hsbc-deploys-wealth-intelligence/); [FStech, 17 Sep 2025](https://www.fstech.co.uk/fst/Hsbc_deploys_gen_ai_proprietary_platform_to_boost_customer_service.php)
- What it does: analyses and summarises Chief Investment Office and other HSBC research plus external news feeds, drawing on more than 10,000 data sources, and gives "fast and simple access" to research reports. Third-party product information will be added over time. Planned future features are product screening and asset-allocation suggestions — [HSBC Private Bank](https://www.privatebanking.hsbc.com/media-releases-and-news/hsbc-deploys-wealth-intelligence/)
- Guardrail: it does not give investment advice or recommendations to customers. It helps RMs prepare for client conversations — [HSBC Trust Threshold release, 24 Jun 2026](https://www.hsbc.com/news-and-views/news/media-releases/2026/the-trust-threshold-ai-makes-investors-bolder-but-they-want-human-judgement-to-make-decisions)
- Status: live in HK and SG. The June 2026 release says it is "being rolled out across multiple markets" — [HSBC, 24 Jun 2026](https://www.hsbc.com/news-and-views/news/media-releases/2026/the-trust-threshold-ai-makes-investors-bolder-but-they-want-human-judgement-to-make-decisions)
- Metrics: no usage, time-saved or accuracy figures have been published — [HSBC Private Bank](https://www.privatebanking.hsbc.com/media-releases-and-news/hsbc-deploys-wealth-intelligence/)

**W2. Wealth Intelligence – HSBC Malaysia rollout**
- Date: 9 Sep 2026. HSBC Malaysia launched Wealth Intelligence for RMs. In Malaysia it analyses CIO data, market commentary and unit trust fund information, and it is for internal use only with no advice to customers. Country Head Linda Yip is quoted — [Fintech News Malaysia](https://fintechnews.my/60791/wealthtech-malaysia/hsbc-ai-wealth-intelligence-malaysia/). Only secondary sources found (Fintech News Malaysia, plus a NewsBeep repost); no HSBC Malaysia release was located.

**W3. Hyper-personalised wealth management support + RM "decision assistant" (Google Cloud partnership)**
- Date: 17 Jun 2026. This is one of three initial delivery areas in the multi-year Google Cloud partnership. RMs will give tailored guidance using AI-driven insights, and the release says it will help "thousands of HSBC relationship managers provide more proactive, tailored financial support and real-time advice" — [HSBC media release](https://www.hsbc.com/news-and-views/news/media-releases/2026/hsbc-and-google-cloud-announce-transformative-ai-banking-partnership); [Google Cloud Press Corner](https://www.googlecloudpresscorner.com/2026-06-17-HSBC-AND-GOOGLE-CLOUD-ANNOUNCE-TRANSFORMATIVE-AI-BANKING-PARTNERSHIP)
- An AI decision assistant cuts admin and meeting-prep time "from hours to minutes" for thousands of employees — [HSBC media release](https://www.hsbc.com/news-and-views/news/media-releases/2026/hsbc-and-google-cloud-announce-transformative-ai-banking-partnership). The Asian Banker adds that HSBC is building AI structures that codify regulatory procedures for RM decision-making, and that the bank chose wealth because RM capacity and cross-border service set banks apart in Asia — [The Asian Banker](https://www.theasianbanker.com/updates-and-articles/hsbc-expands-google-cloud-ai-partnership-with-100-million-use-case-threshold) (secondary source).
- Technology: Gemini models, the Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform, and forward-deployed Google Cloud and Google DeepMind engineers — [HSBC](https://www.hsbc.com/news-and-views/news/media-releases/2026/hsbc-and-google-cloud-announce-transformative-ai-banking-partnership)
- Status: announced and in delivery. The decision assistant is described as already in use.

**W4. Global AI Centre of Excellence, Singapore (wealth-conversation focus)**
- Date: announced 27/28 Jul 2026 (the HSBC page shows 27 Jul; Disruption Banking says 28 Jul). Opens in H2 2026 with more than 100 AI specialist roles. Initial focus areas are "enhancing customer wealth journey conversations", agentic treasury solutions, AI-enabled digital payments, NLP, data science, AI governance and human-centred design — [HSBC](https://www.hsbc.com/news-and-views/news/hsbc-news-archive/hsbc-to-establish-global-ai-centre-of-excellence-in-singapore); [HSBC Singapore](https://www.about.hsbc.com.sg/news-and-media/hsbc-to-launch-global-ai-centre-of-excellence-in-singapore)
- Part of more than 200 new roles across AI and Wealth, including about 100 more RMs across Premier and Private Banking over two years — [Yahoo Finance SG](https://sg.finance.yahoo.com/news/hsbc-plans-hire-100-ai-190530552.html); [Global Private Banker](https://globalprivatebanker.net/hsbc-to-launch-ai-centre-in-singapore-with-major-hiring-plans/)

**W5. "Trust Threshold" affluent investor AI survey (International Wealth & Premier Banking thought leadership)**
- Date: 24 Jun 2026. 73% of affluent and HNW investors use AI for finance, but only 12% say it is the biggest influence on their investment decisions. 62% get investment ideas from professionals versus 32% from AI, 50% prefer a hybrid AI-plus-adviser model, and 49% say AI makes them more willing to take calculated risks — [HSBC](https://www.hsbc.com/news-and-views/news/media-releases/2026/the-trust-threshold-ai-makes-investors-bolder-but-they-want-human-judgement-to-make-decisions). The Ipsos survey covered about 10,000 affluent investors in 10 markets — [Fintech News Malaysia](https://fintechnews.my/60791/wealthtech-malaysia/hsbc-ai-wealth-intelligence-malaysia/)

**W6. AI-powered investment indices (Global Banking & Markets; used as underlyings for wealth and insurance products)**
- HSBC AI Global Tactical Index (AiGT), 20 Apr 2023. It uses AWS machine learning, rebalances weekly across global equities, gold and bonds, and is distributed exclusively through Ibexis fixed indexed annuities in the US. The business is HSBC GBM — [HSBC USA](https://www.about.us.hsbc.com/newsroom/press-releases/hsbc-launches-ai-powered-equity-index-using-aws); [BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20230420005577/en/HSBC-Launches-AI-Powered-Equity-Index-Using-AWS). Celent/AWS material says Boosted.ai develops and hosts it — [AWS/Celent solution brief](https://pages.awscloud.com/rs/112-TZM-766/images/23-GLOBAL-en-US-celent-other-ardm-hsbc-ai-global-tactical-index-solution-brief-reprint.pdf)
- Earlier AI index family: HSBC AiPEX ("first AI driven index"), AiPEX6, AiPEXAR (with IBM Watson) and AiMAX (AI multi-asset index) — [aipex.gbm.hsbc.com](https://aipex.gbm.hsbc.com/); [aipexar.gbm.hsbc.com](https://aipexar.gbm.hsbc.com/); [aimax.gbm.hsbc.com](https://aimax.gbm.hsbc.com/). Launch dates are not shown in the sources gathered.

**W7. HSBC Asset Management – venture investment in Model ML**
- Date: 11 Aug 2026. HSBC AM invested an undisclosed amount through its flagship venture capital strategy, a fund-of-funds programme within its US$81bn Alternatives platform. Model ML is a London agentic-AI workflow platform for banks and asset managers covering research, due diligence, financial modelling and documents — [UKTN](https://www.uktech.news/fintech/hsbc-model-ml-investment-20260811); [Funds Europe](https://funds-europe.com/hsbc-am-invests-in-enterprise-ai-firm-model-ml/)

### Inferences
- Wealth Intelligence is an RM-facing research-synthesis copilot (CIO research plus news), similar in scope to Morgan Stanley's AI @ MS Assistant. As of Sep 2026 it is **not** client-facing. Product screening and asset allocation are still roadmap items, not confirmed live.
- HSBC's wealth AI push is Asia-first (HK, SG, MY). No UK Premier-specific GenAI wealth tool was found.

### Gaps
- Nothing found on automated product-suitability screening going live, hyper-personalised asset allocation for clients, or an AI meeting-notes / call-summarisation tool for wealth RMs specifically. The decision assistant is the closest match.
- Nothing found on RM-copilot deployment in HSBC UK Premier, HSBC Premier outside Asia, or an HSBC Asset Management AI-run ETF or fund.
- FStech names the quoted executive as "Gabriel Castello, chief executive of HSBC Global Private Banking AI". This title looks garbled and was not verified.

## 2. Enterprise GenAI (colleague tools, partnerships, scale)

### Takeaway
About 85% of HSBC employees have GenAI tools, and more than 20,000 developers use coding assistants (15% coding efficiency gain; code and vulnerability patching "five times faster"). HSBC reports more than 600 AI use cases and about 100 or more active GenAI use cases. It runs a dual-vendor model: self-hosted Mistral (Dec 2025) and Google Cloud Gemini (Jun 2026). No Microsoft Copilot deployment could be verified.

### Itemised inventory

**E1. Mistral AI strategic partnership**
- Date: 1 Dec 2025. Multi-year access to Mistral's commercial models, including future ones, **self-hosted on HSBC infrastructure**. HSBC and Mistral teams will co-develop solutions. Use cases: client communications and marketing content, financial analysis for complex lending, multilingual translation and reasoning, faster prototyping, and in future credit/lending, onboarding and fraud/AML. Expected to save employees time on document-heavy tasks from credit assessments to compliance reviews — [HSBC media release](https://www.hsbc.com/news-and-views/news/media-releases/2025/hsbc-and-mistral-ai-join-forces-to-accelerate-ai-adoption-across-global-bank); [PDF](https://www.hsbc.com/-/files/hsbc/media/media-release/2025/251201-hsbc-and-mistral-ai-join-forces-to-accelerate-ai-adoption-across-global-bank.pdf?download=1); [Mistral customer page](https://mistral.ai/customers/hsbc/)
- No adoption metrics were given — [HSBC](https://www.hsbc.com/news-and-views/news/hsbc-news-archive/we-re-partnering-with-ai-powerhouse-mistral)

**E2. Google Cloud multi-year AI partnership (expanded)**
- Date: 17 Jun 2026. More than 200 new AI use cases are expected over two years. Only use cases worth more than US$100m in revenue or efficiency are prioritised. More than 600 HSBC applications already run on Google Cloud. Three initial areas: wealth, financial crime, and AI-empowered teams — [HSBC](https://www.hsbc.com/news-and-views/news/media-releases/2026/hsbc-and-google-cloud-announce-transformative-ai-banking-partnership); [The Asian Banker](https://www.theasianbanker.com/updates-and-articles/hsbc-expands-google-cloud-ai-partnership-with-100-million-use-case-threshold); [PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/hsbc-and-google-cloud-announce-transformative-ai-banking-partnership-302802395.html)

**E3. Colleague GenAI access and in-house LLM productivity tool**
- 85% of employees have access to GenAI tools (Elhedery, FY2025 results call, late Feb 2026) — [Banking Dive, 27 Feb 2026](https://www.bankingdive.com/news/hsbc-generative-ai-leading-investment-elhedery/813353/)
- An LLM-based group productivity tool handles translation, document analysis and text help — [HSBC "Transforming HSBC with AI"](https://www.hsbc.com/who-we-are/hsbc-and-digital/hsbc-and-ai/transforming-hsbc-with-ai). The Mistral release mentions "an AI-powered platform used by colleagues globally to help with productivity tasks" — [HSBC](https://www.hsbc.com/news-and-views/news/hsbc-news-archive/we-re-partnering-with-ai-powerhouse-mistral)
- The CTO role (Mario Shamtani) was expanded to build a central AI platform that gives staff access to models — [HSBC, 23 Mar 2026](https://www.hsbc.com/news-and-views/news/media-releases/2026/david-rice-announced-as-chief-ai-officer)

**E4. Developer coding assistants**
- More than 20,000 developers use coding assistants, with a 15% efficiency gain in coding time — [HSBC "Transforming HSBC with AI"](https://www.hsbc.com/who-we-are/hsbc-and-digital/hsbc-and-ai/transforming-hsbc-with-ai)
- Code and vulnerability patching is "five times faster" (Elhedery) — [Banking Dive](https://www.bankingdive.com/news/hsbc-generative-ai-leading-investment-elhedery/813353/)
- The vendor was not disclosed.

**E5. Harvey AI – legal**
- Date: 20 Jan 2026. Strategic partnership and pilot of Harvey in Group Legal for contract analysis, due diligence, compliance and litigation support. No metrics given — [HSBC](https://www.hsbc.com/news-and-views/news/media-releases/2026/hsbc-announces-harvey-ai-for-their-legal-platform)

**E6. Use-case counts**
- More than 600 AI use cases across the group; more than 100 active GenAI use cases in the 2025 Strategic Report — [CEFPRO](https://connect.cefpro.com/article/view/hsbc-generative-ai-strategy-accelerates-global-banking-transformation); [HSBC "Transforming HSBC with AI"](https://www.hsbc.com/who-we-are/hsbc-and-digital/hsbc-and-ai/transforming-hsbc-with-ai); [The Asian Banker](https://www.theasianbanker.com/updates-and-articles/hsbc-expands-google-cloud-ai-partnership-with-100-million-use-case-threshold)
- At the 1H26 results (Aug 2026) HSBC cited "multiple hundred [AI] use cases, either in production or at pilot stage" — [Disruption Banking, 7 Aug 2026](https://www.disruptionbanking.com/2026/08/07/hsbc-growing-across-all-our-indicators/)
- About 50 processes are being redesigned with AI, including fraud detection and credit applications — [Banking Dive](https://www.bankingdive.com/news/hsbc-generative-ai-leading-investment-elhedery/813353/)

### Inferences
- The dual-vendor setup (self-hosted Mistral for data-sensitive work, Google Gemini for cloud-scale and agentic work) plus OpenAI under Wealth Intelligence means HSBC is multi-model. That makes it a strong baseline for "breadth of partnerships".

### Gaps
- No verified HSBC Microsoft 365 Copilot rollout was found. Searches return only Barclays' Copilot rollout. No Anthropic or AWS Bedrock GenAI deal for HSBC was found.
- No named internal assistant brand was found, and no group-wide hours-saved figure was published.

## 3. Customer-facing AI, financial crime, commercial/markets

### Takeaway
HSBC's strongest documented AI result is **Dynamic Risk Assessment**, co-developed with Google (AML AI). It finds 2 to 4 times more financial crime with 60% fewer false positives across about 1bn transactions a month. Customer-facing GenAI is still limited to UK agent chat summaries, a GenAI servicing assistant in corporate banking, and HKMA sandbox trials. The Amy chatbot in Hong Kong is a 2017-era NLP bot.

### Itemised inventory

**C1. Dynamic Risk Assessment (AML), with Google Cloud AML AI**
- Piloted 2021; Google made it commercially available in 2022. Monitors about 980m transactions a month. Detects 2 to 4 times more financial crime, produces 60% fewer false positives, and cuts analysis time from weeks to days — [HSBC Views, Jennifer Calvery, 10 Jun 2024](https://www.hsbc.com/news-and-views/views/hsbc-views/harnessing-the-power-of-ai-to-fight-financial-crime); [World Bank AI Repository](https://airepository.worldbank.org/use-case/hsbc-dynamic-risk-assessment-aml-fraud-detection)
- Next step (Jun 2026): GenAI and agentic AI for a financial-crime architecture that detects risk earlier across about 1bn monthly transactions, aiming to intervene twice as fast — [HSBC](https://www.hsbc.com/news-and-views/news/media-releases/2026/hsbc-and-google-cloud-announce-transformative-ai-banking-partnership)

**C2. HSBC UK WPB – GenAI chat summaries for customer support agents**
- GenAI gives agents chat summaries, improving service quality and reducing waits. Live; no launch date or metrics published — [HSBC "Transforming HSBC with AI"](https://www.hsbc.com/who-we-are/hsbc-and-digital/hsbc-and-ai/transforming-hsbc-with-ai)
- Contact-centre GenAI tools were cited on the FY25 call — [Banking Dive](https://www.bankingdive.com/news/hsbc-generative-ai-leading-investment-elhedery/813353/)

**C3. CIB GenAI servicing assistant**
- Supports about 3m client interactions a year with 88% client satisfaction — [HSBC "Transforming HSBC with AI"](https://www.hsbc.com/who-we-are/hsbc-and-digital/hsbc-and-ai/transforming-hsbc-with-ai)

**C4. CIB GenAI credit-analysis write-ups**
- Reduces credit application processing time. No figure given — [HSBC "Transforming HSBC with AI"](https://www.hsbc.com/who-we-are/hsbc-and-digital/hsbc-and-ai/transforming-hsbc-with-ai)

**C5. HSBC AI Markets (GBM)**
- Launched 2023 for institutional investors and later extended to large corporates. It is an NLP interface to HSBC cross-asset data and analytics, with an FX chatbot and "Chat-2-Trade" execution. Available through Evolve, third-party platforms and API, and built on in-house NLP/ML. Hundreds of buy-side institutions use it — [HSBC AI Markets product page](https://www.business.hsbc.com/en-gb/products/hsbc-ai-markets); [The TRADE](https://www.thetradenews.com/hsbc-launches-new-artificial-intelligence-global-markets-service-for-institutional-investors/); [FinTech Futures](https://www.fintechfutures.com/ai-in-fintech/hsbc-unveils-ai-markets-for-institutional-investors-and-corporates)

**C6. HKMA GenA.I. Sandbox – real-time customer interaction chatbot trials (Hong Kong)**
- 26 Aug 2025. Internal experiments showed a GenAI chatbot could handle customer interactions on its own, without escalation to a human, and give personalised insights. A public showcase with HKMA was planned for Q4 2025. Status: trial, not live — [HSBC HK](https://www.about.hsbc.com.hk/news-and-media/hsbc-pioneers-real-time-customer-interaction-trials-in-hkmas-genai-sandbox)

**C7. Amy virtual assistant (Hong Kong)**
- Launched around 2017/18. An NLP chatbot answering basic questions such as account opening — [SCMP](https://www.scmp.com/business/companies/article/2128179/hsbc-amy-and-other-soon-be-released-ai-chatbots-are-about-change). It is not GenAI, and its current status was not verified.

**C8. Trade finance document processing (GTRF)**
- HSBC and IBM built a cognitive solution to automate documentary trade checking. GTRF handles more than US$500bn of documentary trade and more than 100m pages a year. The date is not confirmed and the summarised search result may be dated — [IBM case study reference](https://www.ibm.com/case-studies/hsbc-usa?); single, unverified source.

**C9. ValidiFI (US cards)**
- Oct 2025. Account validation and fraud monitoring for credit card payments using data intelligence. AI is not explicit — [PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/hsbc-selects-validifi-to-power-next-generation-bank-account-validation--fraud-monitoring-302594284.html)

### Gaps
- No client-facing GenAI assistant is live in the HSBC UK or HK apps (as of Sep 2026). No published metrics for AI scam or APP-fraud detection in HSBC UK. No AI credit-decisioning metrics for retail.

## 4. Achievements, leadership, governance, research, ventures

### Takeaway
HSBC ranked #8 in the 2025 Evident AI Index, the top UK bank (Lloyds #15, NatWest #16, Barclays #23). It appointed its first Chief AI Officer (David Rice) from 1 Apr 2026, calls GenAI its largest new technology investment, publishes Principles for the Ethical Use of Data and AI, and reported a quantum-plus-AI bond-trading result with IBM.

### Cited Findings
- **Evident AI Index 2025 (8 Oct 2025): HSBC #8**, the first UK-headquartered bank in the top 10. Lloyds is #15 ("second-best in the UK behind HSBC"), NatWest #16, Barclays #23. Citi made the most AI investments of the 50 banks tracked — [Evident Banking Brief](https://evidentinsights.com/bankingbrief/heres-the-2025-evident-ai-index/); [Finadium](https://finadium.com/evident-j-p-morgan-retains-top-spot-for-ai-maturity-but-hsbc-surges/)
- **Chief AI Officer:** David Rice, previously COO of Corporate & Institutional Banking, announced 23 Mar 2026 and effective 1 Apr 2026. It is the first such role at HSBC. CTO Mario Shamtani's remit was expanded at the same time — [HSBC](https://www.hsbc.com/news-and-views/news/media-releases/2026/david-rice-announced-as-chief-ai-officer); [Banking Dive](https://www.bankingdive.com/news/hsbc-david-rice-ai-chief-cto-mario-shamtani-expanded-role-elhedery/815655/)
- **CEO statements:** Elhedery said GenAI is "definitely" the biggest new technology investment, and that it will make "a more productive bank, more efficient bank and a safe bank with stronger controls", with customer experience "materially enhanced" (FY25 results, Feb 2026) — [Banking Dive](https://www.bankingdive.com/news/hsbc-generative-ai-leading-investment-elhedery/813353/). The stated AI vision is to "empower colleagues to use AI to create a personalised experience for each customer… while keeping human judgement, decision-making and accountability at the core" — [HSBC CoE release](https://www.hsbc.com/news-and-views/news/hsbc-news-archive/hsbc-to-establish-global-ai-centre-of-excellence-in-singapore)
- **Cost context:** US$1.8bn of costs reallocated to growth areas including technology and AI. US$1.2bn annualised simplification savings in 2025, with US$1.5bn expected by 1H26 — [CEFPRO / search summary](https://connect.cefpro.com/article/view/hsbc-generative-ai-strategy-accelerates-global-banking-transformation) (secondary). The bank plans to retire 3,000 of its 9,000+ applications by 2028 — [The Asian Banker](https://www.theasianbanker.com/updates-and-articles/hsbc-expands-google-cloud-ai-partnership-with-100-million-use-case-threshold)
- **Governance:** "HSBC's Principles for the Ethical Use of Data and AI" (PDF) — [HSBC and AI](https://www.hsbc.com/who-we-are/hsbc-and-digital/hsbc-and-ai). AI governance frameworks were refined during 1H25 — [HSBC "Transforming HSBC with AI"](https://www.hsbc.com/who-we-are/hsbc-and-digital/hsbc-and-ai/transforming-hsbc-with-ai)
- **Quantum research:** 25 Sep 2025, with IBM. A hybrid quantum-classical approach on an IBM Heron processor gave up to a 34% improvement in predicting whether a bond trade fills at the quoted price. The test used about 1.1m trade requests across more than 5,000 European corporate bonds, and HSBC billed it a "world-first" — [HSBC](https://www.hsbc.com/news-and-views/news/media-releases/2025/hsbc-demonstrates-worlds-first-known-quantum-enabled-algorithmic-trading-with-ibm). Scott Aaronson disputes that it shows quantum advantage — [Aaronson blog](https://scottaaronson.blog/?p=9170)
- **AI venture investments (secondary / search-snippet level, needs verification):** Poolside (AI coding; US$500m round including HSBC), Prophecy (data copilot Series B extension), Classiq (quantum software Series B), and Nova Credit (US$10m, credit data) — [HSBC Ventures news](https://www.ventures.hsbc.com/news). Model ML by HSBC AM is verified (see W7).
- **ML history:** HSBC says it has built ML models for about 10 years — [HSBC and AI](https://www.hsbc.com/who-we-are/hsbc-and-digital/hsbc-and-ai)

### Inferences
- For benchmarking: HSBC leads UK peers on Evident ranking and on disclosed partnerships (Google, Mistral, OpenAI, Harvey, IBM, AWS). It lags on client-facing GenAI in the UK. Its wealth AI remains RM-assist only.

### Gaps
- The 2026 Evident AI Index (usually published in October) is not yet out as of 23 Sep 2026. HSBC's 2024 rank was not captured.
- No specific AI awards for HSBC (e.g. The Banker, Asian Private Banker) were verified.
- Individual HSBC Ventures deal dates and amounts were not confirmed from primary sources.
