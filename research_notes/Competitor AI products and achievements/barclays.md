# Barclays: AI products, deployments and achievements (as of September 2026)

Scope: Barclays Group, with a focus on Private Bank & Wealth Management (PBWM), Barclays UK Premier Wealth Management and Smart Investor (now "Direct Investing"). Compiled 23 Sep 2026 as context for an HSBC wealth competitor benchmark.

## Q1. What GenAI / AI tools has Barclays rolled out to colleagues?

### Takeaway
Barclays' flagship colleague AI programme is Microsoft 365 Copilot. It started as a 15,000-user pilot and was announced on 9 June 2025 for 100,000 colleagues, built into an in-house "colleague productivity tool" with Microsoft-based agents. GitLab Duo is the main coding assistant, with about 23,000 users. Barclays says it runs 250+ AI tools and models across the group.

### Inferences
- Barclays depends heavily on Microsoft for colleague AI (Copilot, Viva and agents), and Copilot is positioned as the single front end for its internal AI. No OpenAI, Google or Anthropic enterprise deal was found.
- The volume of Copilot seats (about 100k) is comparable to large peers. However, Barclays has published no quantified productivity result for Copilot.

### Gaps
- There are no published Copilot productivity metrics (hours saved or cost) and no confirmation that all 100k seats were live by September 2026.
- "250+ AI tools/models" and the credit-risk AI come from one secondary source (Diginomica, reporting the Feb 2026 investor update). I could not open the primary investor update deck to check them.
- I found no named in-house LLM platform and no disclosed foundation-model provider for the Barclays UK colleague assistant.

## Q2. What AI is used in Barclays Wealth / Private Bank / Smart Investor?

### Takeaway
Very little wealth-specific AI has been disclosed publicly. The only concrete use case is AI call summarisation in PBWM, disclosed in July 2025. The big 2026 wealth move is a digital-first advice model, "Premier Wealth Management" / Planning and Advice, launched 30 Apr 2026. It relies on digital provisioning and a Wealth Hub in the app, but Barclays does not describe it as AI-driven. No wealth adviser copilot, AI suitability or AI portfolio tool has been publicly named.

### Inferences
- Barclays' wealth strategy for linking retail customers to high-net-worth advice is a hybrid of human advisers and a digital app, targeting mass-affluent Premier customers. Its public competitive levers are price (custody fees to zero) and structure (advice inside the bank). A visible AI tool is not one of them. This contrasts with banks that promote named adviser copilots.
- Call summarisation in PBWM is the likely seed of an adviser note-taking or copilot capability, but Barclays has not named or sized one.

### Gaps
- No public evidence was found of a Barclays adviser/RM copilot, AI meeting-notes vendor (Aveni, Zocks, Jump, etc.), AI suitability checks, AI portfolio construction or AI-driven next-best-action in wealth.
- The Wealth Hub's features (for example any AI insights) are not described in detail.
- I found no AI features for Smart Investor / Direct Investing.

## Q4. AI achievements: strategy, benefits, partnerships, governance, leadership, sandbox, investments, rankings

### Takeaway
Barclays presents AI as the "icing on the cake" of its cost programme rather than a headline product. It sits behind HSBC in AI maturity rankings (Evident 2025: Barclays #23, HSBC #8). In 2025–26 it made visible ecosystem moves: the Eagle Labs AI Innovation Hub with Microsoft and NVIDIA, the FCA AI Live Testing cohort 2, an investment in CommonAI, a GenAI governance framework, and new AI leadership appointments.

### Cited Findings
**Strategy and stated benefits**
- **FY2025 results and 2028 targets (Feb 2026).** 2025 profit before tax was £9.1bn (+12%). The RoTE target is now above 14% by 2028, and Barclays plans to return more than £15bn to shareholders over 2026–28. Barclays says AI is one lever for keeping costs low and treats it "as a core part of running the business". Named uses include risk analysis, customer-service workflows, internal reporting and repetitive-task automation. Source: [AI News, 11 Feb 2026](https://www.artificialintelligence-news.com/news/barclays-bets-on-ai-to-cut-costs-and-boost-returns/).
- **Cost savings.** Barclays is targeting about £2bn of savings over 2026–28. It saved £700m in 2025, for £1.7bn over two years. Sources: search snippets of [Yahoo Finance / PA](https://uk.finance.yahoo.com/news/barclays-home-ai-profit-jumps-101004503.html) (the page returned 404 when fetched, so these figures come from one secondary source and should be checked).
- **CEO C.S. Venkatakrishnan, 14 Sep 2026.** "AI is a very critical part of... reducing the unit costs of production, service and innovation... AI is the icing on the cake; it's not the cake." The "cake" is four things: workforce tools and adoption, infrastructure (processes, data, compute), a focus on the highest-value use cases, and reskilling.
- **CFO Anna Cross, same event.** AI should reduce cost to serve. Barclays manages the net cost ("tokenomics") and deploys "agentic AI where it's appropriate and machine learning where it's appropriate". Source for both: [Conference transcript](https://home.barclays/content/dam/home-barclays/documents/investor-relations/IRNewsPresentations/2026News/Barclays%2024th%20Annual%20Global%20Financial%20Services%20Conference%202026%20transcript.pdf).
- **CEO at the Feb 2026 update.** "For some time now, technology has revolved around our businesses; now our businesses are revolving around technology." Source: [Diginomica](https://diginomica.com/tale-two-banks-simplification-and-ai-barclays-story).

**Leadership and hires**
- **Craig Bright**, Group CIO, was the spokesperson for the 2025 AI rollout. Sources: [Microsoft UK](https://ukstories.microsoft.com/features/barclays-rolls-out-microsoft-365-copilot-to-100000-colleagues/); [Barclays](https://home.barclays/insights/2025/07/scaling-AI-at-Barclays/).
- **Antoinette O'Neill** (12 Sep 2025) was promoted from Investment Bank COO to Group CIO, with responsibility for AI strategy, starting mid-October 2025. Source: [Bloomberg Law](https://news.bloomberglaw.com/artificial-intelligence/barclays-shifts-top-investment-bank-executive-to-lead-ai-push).
- **Sahana Athreya** (14 Apr 2026) was appointed Global Head of Data Science & Applied AI, Barclays Research (New York). She previously worked at Millennium and Eisler, and her remit includes AI-enabled tools for clients. Source: [Barclays press release](https://home.barclays/news/press-releases/20260/04/barclays-research-appoints-sahana-athreya-as-global-head-of-data/).
- **Jason Tuo**, global head of AI governance since 2023. Source: [Risk.net](https://www.risk.net/risk-management/7963399/barclays-built-a-risk-framework-for-genai-from-scratch) (paywalled; seen via search snippet).

**Governance and Responsible AI**
- **GenAI governance framework.** In Q1 2026 Barclays implemented a firm-wide governance regime built specifically for GenAI. It was developed by Jason Tuo. Source: [Risk.net](https://www.risk.net/risk-management/7963399/barclays-built-a-risk-framework-for-genai-from-scratch) (snippet only).
- **AI Risk Governance function.** The function covers AI policies, standards and operating model with Risk, Compliance, Legal, Technology and Data Science. Source: [Barclays job posting](https://search.jobs.barclays/job/wilmington/vp-ai-risk-governance/13015/97614931424).
- **Principles.** Barclays describes its approach as "pace with purpose" within a framework of responsibility and trust, with a GenAI Centre of Excellence. Source: [Barclays, Jul 2025](https://home.barclays/insights/2025/07/scaling-AI-at-Barclays/).

**Regulatory sandbox**
- **FCA AI Live Testing, cohort 2 (announced about 21 Apr 2026).** Barclays is one of 8 firms alongside UBS, Lloyds (Scottish Widows), Experian, Aereve, Coadjute, GoCardless and Palindrome. Testing runs from April 2026 to year-end with Advai as technical partner, and results are expected in Q1 2027. Barclays' specific use case was not disclosed. Sources: [QA Financial](https://qa-financial.com/barclays-ubs-and-lloyds-join-fcas-live-ai-testing-push-in-uk/); [FStech](https://www.fstech.co.uk/fst/FCA_Expands_AI_Testing_With_Major_Banks_And_FinTechs.php); [Retail Banker International](https://www.retailbankerinternational.com/news/fca-ai-testing-eight-banks/).

**Partnerships, ecosystem and investments**
- **Barclays Innovation Hub, Shoreditch (9 Jun 2025).** Powered by Eagle Labs, with Microsoft, NVIDIA, Databricks, Conception X, Innovate Finance and Twin Path Ventures. It has space for 150 AI, deep-tech and innovation-economy startups. Source: [Barclays press release](https://home.barclays/news/press-releases/2025/06/barclays-joins-forces-with-microsoft-and-nvidia-to-launch-new-in/).
- **Eagle Labs network.** 43 sites and more than 17,000 businesses supported. Its Scaleup Consortium includes Google Cloud. Sources: same release; [Eagle Labs](https://labs.uk.barclays/).
- **Eagle Labs report on UK AI scaleups (May 2026).** Source: [Barclays press release](https://home.barclays/news/press-releases/20260/050/barclays-eagle-labs-unveils-report-profiling-the-uk-s-next-gener/) (title only; not opened).
- **Investment in CommonAI (9 Jun 2026).** CommonAI is a UK/European collaborative AI compute and engineering platform founded by Anthemis and Cambridge AI Venture Partners. Barclays joins its High Assurance AI programme, which works on accurate, explainable AI for regulated sectors. The amount was not disclosed. Source: [Barclays press release](https://home.barclays/news/press-releases/20260/060/barclays-backs-uk-ai-innovation-with-investment-in-commonai/).
- **Vendors confirmed from primary or vendor sources:** Microsoft (Copilot, Viva) and GitLab (Duo). NVIDIA and Databricks are named as hub partners only. Sources: as above.

**Rankings**
- **Evident AI Index 2025.** Barclays ranked #23 of 50, behind HSBC (#8), Lloyds (#15) and NatWest (#16). Source: [Evident, 2025 index](https://evidentinsights.com/bankingbrief/heres-the-2025-evident-ai-index/).

### Inferences
- HSBC has a clear lead in AI maturity (Evident #8 vs #23). Barclays' strengths are scaled colleague AI (Copilot and GitLab Duo), measured cost programmes and UK ecosystem and regulatory work. Its weakness for a wealth benchmark is that it has no visible client-facing or adviser-facing AI in wealth.
- The Group CIO changed in October 2025 (Craig Bright to Antoinette O'Neill), and AI strategy moved under an executive from the Investment Bank. This suggests AI is being run as an operating-efficiency programme.

### Gaps
- There is no 2026 Evident ranking for Barclays and no Barclays 2024 ranking in the pages I opened.
- I found no AI awards won by Barclays in 2025–26.
- There is no disclosed AI investment amount (CommonAI) and no Barclays Rise items specific to AI in 2025–26.
- The quantified AI benefit in the FY2025 annual report or investor update could not be checked from primary documents.
- No OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini or AWS Bedrock enterprise partnership was found in public sources.
