## Q1. AI financial assistant / conversational & agentic features in the app

### Takeaway
Revolut announced an AI "financial companion" in November 2024. It launched as **AIR (AI by Revolut)** to 13M UK customers on 9 April 2026. AIR is a conversational layer over banking and investing: spending insights, investment tracking, subscriptions, card controls and eSIM purchase. Sensitive actions need biometric approval, and Revolut says a zero-data-retention policy applies with its undisclosed third-party LLM providers. No "AI private banker" product has been launched. Private banking (£500k threshold) was reported for summer 2026 with an AI-enhanced investment service planned, but no confirmed launch was found by Sept 2026.

## Q2. Wealth & Trading AI (Robo-Advisor, insights, Revolut X, private banking)

### Takeaway
Revolut's most advanced agentic AI work in wealth is in **crypto**. In July 2026 Revolut X (its standalone exchange) opened an MCP-based integration. It lets third-party assistants (Claude, Gemini, Cursor, OpenClaw) analyse markets, monitor portfolios, set alerts, backtest and *stage* orders, but every order still needs human confirmation. In stocks, the AI-related items are rules-based: the Robo-Advisor (US 2023, EEA 2024), "Suggestions" buy/hold/sell views, and portfolio and market summaries via AIR. A private bank with a £500k threshold and new FCA permissions (March 2026) is the wealth push, with AI "to be integrated" into investing.

## Q3. Fraud/scam AI, AI customer support, KYC, credit

### Takeaway
Fraud is Revolut's longest-running production AI area: the Sherlock card-fraud ML model (around 2019), ML-based APP scam detection, an AI card-scam detector with a claimed 30% drop in card-scam investment-fraud losses, and PRAGMA-driven fraud gains. Support is AI-first: a chatbot resolving about 75% of queries, plus ElevenLabs voice agents (Jan 2026) serving 4M+ customers in 31+ languages with 8x faster resolution.

**Item 9: Sherlock (card-fraud ML)**

## Q4. AI achievements, research, leadership and licence context

### Takeaway
Revolut's biggest AI achievement is **PRAGMA**, its own banking foundation model (arXiv April 2026, up to 1B parameters, trained on 24–40B events from about 26M users in 111 countries on NVIDIA H100s via Nebius). On 25 August 2026 it became the core of a new **Revolut Research** division. The context is a full UK banking licence (11 March 2026), 70M+ customers, and £4.5bn revenue in 2025.

### Cited Findings

**Item 15: PRAGMA foundation model**
- arXiv paper "PRAGMA: Revolut Foundation Model" submitted 9 Apr 2026 (v2 24 Aug 2026). It is a Transformer encoder with masked modelling on heterogeneous banking event sequences, for credit scoring, fraud detection and lifetime-value prediction — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.08649)
- Scale: models from tens of millions to 1B parameters. Training used about 26M users, 111 countries, 24–40B events and 207B tokens over about 28 months of history. Hardware was NVIDIA H100 on Nebius AI Cloud; merchant text is embedded with NVIDIA Llama-Nemotron-Embed-1B-v2; LoRA fine-tuning is used — [NVIDIA case study](https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/case-studies/revolut/). Finextra cites about 25M users — [Finextra blog](https://www.finextra.com/blogposting/31540/deep-dive-revoluts-pragma---the-transaction-foundation-model)
- Results on historical data: 2.3x better default-risk identification; 65% more fraud caught (64.7% recall gain) with 17% higher precision; 41% more relevant product recommendations; 3–5x faster model development — [NVIDIA](https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/case-studies/revolut/)
- The fraud-screening latency figure differs by source: NVIDIA says sub-second; older Sherlock figures say 50ms.

**Item 16: Revolut Research (AI research division)**
- Launched 25 Aug 2026 inside Revolut's AI Department and built around PRAGMA. It supports real-time risk assessment, operations and product recommendations, and works with NVIDIA and academic partners — [Revolut newsroom](https://www.revolut.com/news/revolut_announces_launch_of_dedicated_ai_research_division_revolut_research/); [Finextra](https://www.finextra.com/newsarticle/48296/revolut-launches-dedicated-ai-research-lab-built-on-its-own-foundation-model); [Retail Banker International](https://www.retailbankerinternational.com/news/revolut-ai-focused-research-division/); [TechAfrica News](https://techafricanews.com/2026/08/26/revolut-launches-research-division-to-advance-ai-powered-financial-services/)

**Item 17: Leadership and AI organisation**
- The dedicated AI unit hires for personal assistants, voice, call-centre and outbound sales agents. Storonsky and Yatsenko fronted the Nov 2024 announcement — [Sifted](https://sifted.eu/articles/revolut-ai-agents-voice-calls); [Sifted](https://sifted.eu/articles/revolut-ai-assistant)

Revolut has 13M UK customers — [Tech.eu](https://tech.eu/2026/03/11/revolut-wins-full-uk-banking-licence-as-finally-exits-mobilisation-phase/); [Revolut newsroom](https://www.revolut.com/news/revolut_launches_uk_bank/)

### Inferences
- Compared with HSBC wealth, Revolut's AI edge is **distribution plus a proprietary data model**. PRAGMA gives it one customer-behaviour backbone for fraud, credit and recommendations. The advisory/wealth AI itself is still early: AIR does tracking, Robo-Advisor is rules-based, and the private bank is not yet confirmed launched.
- Revolut's most agentic customer-facing feature, MCP-connected trading, sits in crypto (Revolut X), where regulation is lighter. It keeps a human-in-the-loop order confirmation. Extending this to regulated stock and ISA investing would be the next signal to watch.
- 2026 is when Revolut's AI went into production across the board: voice agents (Jan), full licence (Mar), PRAGMA paper and AIR (Apr), private bank plans (May), Revolut X MCP (Jul), Revolut Research (Aug).
