01

Executive summary

HSBC leads UK banks on overall AI maturity and on financial-crime AI, but it trails in wealth. Our only live wealth AI is an RM research copilot in three Asian markets, with no published usage figures. Citi has shipped seven named wealth AI tools, including a client-facing avatar, and Lloyds, Revolut and NatWest already put AI assistants in front of UK customers.

Where HSBC leads

  • External maturity. We ranked #8 in the 2025 Evident AI Index, the top UK bank (Lloyds #15, NatWest #16, Barclays #23).
  • Financial crime. Dynamic Risk Assessment detects 2–4× more financial crime with 60% fewer false positives across ~1bn transactions a month. No peer has disclosed a stronger result.
  • Partner breadth. Google Cloud (Gemini and DeepMind engineers), self-hosted Mistral, OpenAI in Wealth Intelligence, and Harvey.
  • Asia RM copilot. Wealth Intelligence has been live in HK and SG since Sep 2025 and in Malaysia since Sep 2026. A Singapore AI Centre of Excellence focused on wealth conversations opens in H2 2026.

Where HSBC lags

  • No client-facing wealth AI. Peers have Citi Sky, Lloyds InvestAI (and the Financial Assistant at 500k+ customers), Revolut AIR (13M UK customers) and NatWest's agentic Cora. We have HKMA sandbox trials.
  • A narrower RM toolkit. We have no meeting-notes tool, unlike CitiScribe or NatWest's 15 minutes saved per meeting. Product screening and asset allocation are still on the roadmap.
  • No UK wealth AI. All our disclosed wealth AI is in Asia.
  • No quantified value. Lloyds reports £50m in 2025 and targets £100m+ in 2026, JPMorgan reports ~$1.5bn, and NatWest reports ~£100m of capacity freed. We publish none.

What it means

  • The UK mass-affluent window is closing. FCA targeted support has applied since April 2026. Lloyds is using it to target 1m new investors through AI.
  • Capability isn't what's holding us back. Citi built Citi Sky on the same Google Cloud stack we signed in June 2026. What's missing is a decision and a rollout plan.
  • Our hybrid thesis has data behind it. In our Trust Threshold survey, 50% of affluent investors prefer AI plus an adviser, and only 12% say AI is their biggest influence. That supports client-facing AI that hands off to an RM.
  • We need to measure what we have. Without usage and time-saved data for Wealth Intelligence, we can't defend our position.

What the matrix shows

Peers are live across the whole adviser workflow, from research and meeting notes to client prep and conduct review. HSBC covers research only. On the client side, five firms have a live assistant or portfolio-insight product, and HSBC has sandbox trials.

The enterprise rows are close to even. HSBC is at parity on colleague and coding tools and leads on financial crime. So the gap is in wealth products, not in the platform underneath them.

How to read it

A cell shows a firm's named product for that capability, with its scope. "None disclosed" means we found no public evidence, not that the capability doesn't exist internally. Rules-based robo services are counted as none, because they aren't AI.

The J.P. Morgan column is group-level. None of those products is confirmed at JPMPI in the UK. Barclays' US cards chatbot is excluded because it isn't UK or wealth.

Glossary

Agentic AI
AI that plans and carries out multi-step tasks with tools, rather than only answering questions.
Evident AI Index
An annual ranking of 50 banks on AI maturity across talent, innovation, leadership and transparency.
FCA AI Live Testing
The FCA programme for testing AI with real consumers under supervision. Cohort 2 runs Apr–Dec 2026.
Targeted support
A new FCA regime (PS25/22, from April 2026) that lets firms give ready-made suggestions to groups of consumers without full advice.
MCP
Model Context Protocol, a standard way for AI assistants to connect to external tools and data such as a trading API.
RAG
Retrieval-augmented generation: grounding an LLM's answers in approved internal documents.
DRA
Dynamic Risk Assessment, HSBC's AML model built with Google Cloud AML AI.
CIO
Chief Investment Office: the house view and research function in a wealth business.
